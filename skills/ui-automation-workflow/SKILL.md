# ui-automation-workflow

## 概要
Capture・Record・Assist・TestのModeを選択し、成果物を引き渡します。自動Recoveryは担当しません。

## Purpose
Coordinate FlowUI modes safely.

## Procedure
Observe, choose a mode, validate artifacts, and stop with a machine-readable reason when blocked.

## Rules
Do not retry mutations automatically.
